Breast Cancer Treatment Response Prediction Using Longitudinal MRI and Clinical Data University of Nottingham - Computer Sci
Sept 2023 – Dec 2025
  • Developed predictive modelling framework analysing longitudinal MRI scans (1,700+ scans, 600 patients) to forecast pathological treatment response (PCR) and relapse-free survival (RFS)
  • Engineered an automated feature extraction pipeline combining radiomic analysis and deep learning representations (pretrained networks), reducing manual feature engineering while capturing temporal tumour morphology changes
